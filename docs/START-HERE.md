# Clinic Stock — start here

---

## Today, first thing (do this once)

1. Open the app.
2. Close it.
3. Open it again.

That is all. The app updated itself. Nothing you entered before is lost.

---

## Giving a vaccine

1. Tap the vaccine box.
2. Tap the child's name. Or tap **Skip, no name**.
3. Tap **Give 1 dose**.

The count goes down straight away.

Tapped the wrong one? Tap **Undo** on the black bar.

---

## The child's age

1. Tap the child's name, or type it.
2. Roll the three wheels to the day, month and year of birth.
3. Tap **Give 1 dose**.

The age shows on every dose. Next time it fills in by itself.

---

## New stock has come

1. Tap **Add stock** at the top.
2. Tap the vaccine.
3. Type the batch number from the box.
4. Roll the wheels to the expiry month and year.
5. Tap **Bought** or **Government (free)**.
6. Tap **−** or **+** for how many vials came.
7. Tap **Add to stock**.

You do not need to multiply. The app does it.

---

## Check this once, before you trust the numbers

1. Tap **Vaccines** at the top.
2. Look at each vaccine you use.
3. Check the doses per vial matches the real vial in your fridge.

If this number is wrong, every total for that vaccine will be wrong.

---

## Save a copy

1. Tap **More** at the top.
2. Tap **Back up / restore**.
3. Tap **Back up now**.
4. Send the file to yourself on WhatsApp.

Do this today. Then do it whenever the app asks.

---

## Things to remember

**Blue** takes stock out. **Green** puts stock in. Wrong colour means wrong tab.

**You never need a child's name.** Never stop because you do not have it.

**Nothing is ever deleted.** You can fix a mistake days later.

**Everything saves by itself.** You do not need to press anything to save.

---

## If you see a bar at the top

| Bar | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Yellow, asking you to back up | Tap **Back up**. Or **Later**. |
| Yellow or red, about entries waiting | Nothing. Check the internet. Your work is safe on the phone. |
| Red message on a screen | Read it. It tells you if anything was saved. |

---

## Something looks wrong

1. Tap **More**.
2. Tap **All entries**.
3. Find the wrong one.
4. Tap **Correct this entry**.

Or send a photo of the screen on WhatsApp.
